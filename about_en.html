WHO THE F*CK IS LITRÄO?







Liträo is a sludge/doom metal band from Rio de Janeiro, Brazil. Formed in 2017 by four friends who had previously played together in former projects, the group is heavily influenced by bands like Sleep, Crowbar and Electric Wizard. The lineup is Guilherme Kirk (guitar/vocals), Guilherme Stephano (solo guitar), Gabriel Fontes (bass) and Marcos Soli (drums).
